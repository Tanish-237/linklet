# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

Linklet is built by and for the MNNIT Allahabad student community. We want contributing to this project — and using the platform it powers — to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback on code and ideas.
- Focusing on what's best for the community and the platform's users.
- Showing empathy toward other contributors and students using the platform.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information (a real name, email, or any personal detail) without explicit permission.
- Trolling, or deliberately derailing technical discussions.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (this repository, its issues and pull requests) and when representing the project in public spaces on campus or online.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported via the [Contact page](https://linklet.org/contact) or by emailing [founderslinklet@gmail.com](mailto:founderslinklet@gmail.com). All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
